Frequently Asked Questions

Can you build from only Google Maps?

Yes, with owner approval and missing details supplied. We extract what's publicly available and ask you to fill gaps.

Do I need hosting?

We can manage it for you or hand it over. Our monthly plans include hosting management, or you can self-host the static files.

What changes are included?

One small correction round is included with the Proof-Builder website. The hosting plan includes up to 15 minutes of minor edits per month. Extra pages or larger changes are handled as listed website add-ons.

Is AI included?

AI tools may help us build and check your site faster, but the only public AI services currently offered are the AI Task-Mapping Taster and Tailored Team AI Workflow Workshop. They are separate premium services, NOT included in standard website builds.

What is the AI workshop?

A practical session for one team. We map what they actually do, choose suitable AI tools, show safe-use rules, and build examples they can use immediately. It is not a generic AI awareness course.

Which AI training should we choose?

Use the AI Task-Mapping Taster if you want a quick 60–90 minute starting point for up to 10 people. Use the Tailored Team AI Workflow Workshop if you want a deeper 2-hour session for up to 20 people with prompt packs, workflow templates, safe-use guardrails and 14 days email support.

Do you guarantee results?

Yes — with a 100% money-back guarantee. If your new site doesn't match or beat your previous 60-day results (you share your old analytics), you get a full refund. New businesses with no prior site: if the site produces zero enquiries in 60 days, full refund. We improve clarity, trust, speed, contact paths and tracking. Leads still depend on your offer, demand and traffic, but we stand fully behind our work.

How long does it take?

Proof-Builder websites normally take 3–5 working days. AI Task-Mapping Tasters are usually within 5 working days after the questionnaire, and Tailored Team AI Workflow Workshops usually take 7–10 working days from intake. Timeline depends on how quickly you provide details and approve the preview.

What if I don't have reviews yet?

We build with what you have. If you're new, we focus on service clarity, about section, and strong contact path. We can also set up a review request system as an add-on.

Can you help with Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google Business Profile polish is available as an add-on at £95 to update photos, services, description, Q&A, and attributes.
